Host: It changes model-selection governance. Procurement, risk and engineering teams need a repeatable way to approve the cheapest model that is good enough for a task, while keeping higher-risk work on stronger models with stricter review.

Co-host: It also changes vendor negotiation. If a bank can prove that routine summarization, classification or code-support work runs safely on smaller internal models, it has more leverage when a vendor prices every workflow as though it needs the flagship model.
