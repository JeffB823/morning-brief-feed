Co-host: That is more concrete than a demo, but the proof still needs outside mathematical review.

Co-host: The capability implication is that frontier systems are moving from answering hard questions to organizing many agents around a research program. Host: The earliest bank use is not autonomous strategy. It is contained research acceleration for model-risk, quant, legal, or engineering teams, gated by source provenance, expert signoff, and a formal record of what the agents read, wrote, and changed.
