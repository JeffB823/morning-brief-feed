HOST: The practical consequence is planning. States and counties may need larger emergency reserves, tighter documentation, and faster mutual-aid agreements because federal timing is less predictable. For households, the lesson is not to assume federal aid arrives quickly or covers everything. Insurance, document storage, local assistance, and temporary housing plans matter.

COHOST: The counter-signal is that disaster requests vary. A denial can reflect damage thresholds, documentation quality, or legal criteria, not only partisan treatment.

HOST: The practical consequence is simple but specific. If someone has persistent watery diarrhea, cramping, fatigue, or symptoms that improve and then return after eating suspect produce, they should contact a clinician and ask whether Cyclospora testing is appropriate. For restaurants and food-service buyers, the operational issue is supply-chain verification: which lettuce, which supplier, which lot, which distribution path, and whether recalled product is fully out of inventory.
