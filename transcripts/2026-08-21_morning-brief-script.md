If a street, park, housing project, or public-safety issue is concentrated in one area, voters know exactly who owns it. The argument for at-large seats is that Columbus also has citywide problems: housing supply, transit, utilities, growth, tax policy, and infrastructure that cross district lines.

Freddie Mac's survey averages application data from the prior Thursday through Wednesday, so it can lag the daily market. If the 10-year Treasury stays near 4.7 percent and oil keeps inflation pressure alive, retail mortgage quotes can reverse even after the weekly survey improves.
